## 1. Purpose

Ω-Cognitive Fabric is the capability-engineering layer of VX. It does not treat a model name as a capability. It discovers required capabilities, composes minds and tools, runs controlled experiments, subjects compositions to adversarial evaluation, verifies outcomes, and promotes only reproducible capabilities into the reusable Capability Graph.

## 2. System boundary

VAIXLNS → VLNS → VX → evidence/proof → registry/evolution.

- VAIXLNS: canonical authority, governance, recovery, provenance, master registry.
- VLNS: model/knowledge activation and semantic intelligence fabric; it prepares, selects, activates, and mediates model participation through governed contracts.
- VX: execution, orchestration, experimentation, verification, replay, capability synthesis, and governed runtime.
- NEXENT/NEXNET: evidence/discovery federation surfaces remain lineage-controlled until identity mappings are proven.

The historical repository-to-system mappings must not be silently rewritten. Any new VLNS role is recorded as a specification/proposal until repository evidence proves the corresponding implementation.

## 5. Capability state machine

OBSERVATION → PATTERN → HYPOTHESIS → CAPABILITY_CANDIDATE → CONTROLLED_EXPERIMENT → VERIFIED_CAPABILITY → ADOPTED_CAPABILITY → REUSABLE_PRIMITIVE

Capability states:
HYPOTHESIZED, SPECIFIED, PROTOTYPED, TESTED, VERIFIED, ADOPTED, DEPRECATED, QUARANTINED.

A successful answer alone never establishes a VERIFIED capability.

## 6. Capability separation

MODEL ≠ MIND ≠ CAPABILITY ≠ AUTHORITY

A model may propose a capability. VX may verify a capability. Governance separately determines whether the capability may be used and under which permissions.

## 7. Capability lineage

Every derived capability retains parent capabilities, evidence chains, verification chains, replay chains, and provenance. A capability may be revoked or quarantined when later evidence invalidates an assumption.

## 8. No permanent winner

Performance is conditional:

Performance = f(capability, task, context, constraints, strategy, tools, evidence, time)

Ω-ARENA therefore produces conditional capability results, not an absolute claim that one model is universally superior.

## 9. Innovation extraction

Repeated successful compositions may produce a Capability Candidate. Promotion requires independent tests, reproducibility, adversarial evaluation, safety review, and governance approval.

## 10. Model participation

Models enter VX only after the VX protocol and evaluation environment are established. Candidate participants may include OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, Claude Code, and future providers. The provider is an adapter; the capability contract is provider-independent.

## 11. Immutable tournament rule

Once an Ω-ARENA evaluation is admitted, its protocol, scoring contract, task corpus, environment, and authority boundaries are frozen for that evaluation window. A participant cannot alter the rules used to evaluate itself.

## 12. Evidence rule

No score is authoritative without a traceable Evidence Event. A verdict must explain:
- which tests passed/failed;
- which evidence supports the result;
- whether the result is reproducible;
- which claims were unsupported or contradicted;
- which execution and replay records produced the score.

## 13. Self-audit

VX is itself auditable. An external or independent auditor may attack the judge, evidence, scoring, replay, experiment design, capability extraction, and governance decision.
