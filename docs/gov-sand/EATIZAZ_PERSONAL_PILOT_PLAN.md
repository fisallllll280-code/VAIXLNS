# Pilot through Eatizaz — proposed founder self-test
Status: NOT SUBMITTED / NOT AUTHORIZED.

## Sequence
A. Private local self-test with **invented numbers only**: see tools/gov_sand_case_triage.py. The founder may evaluate UX safely without involving Defence.
B. Verify eligibility for Eatizaz membership and the official partner path. Official site: https://eatizaz.mod.gov.sa/homepage . Being a founder does not itself establish beneficiary or partner eligibility.
C. Request an introductory meeting with the appropriate programme office: propose a demonstration **not** involving live payroll, court, banking or personnel data.
D. If officials permit a pilot, complete registration/entity authorization, data protection/security reviews, explicit voluntary consent and agreement on scope.
E. A real personal case may be processed only in an approved secure environment, never via public GitHub or a public demo.
F. Independent human assessment of outcomes and STOP criteria.

## Acceptance criteria
- Before live pilot: written sponsor and authorization, data processing roles, lawful purpose, retention/deletion and complaints process, red-team/security checks, audit visibility.
- Pilot measured by paperwork completeness and response time, not by automatic financial approval, debt cancellation or court exemption.
- Eligible volunteer can withdraw; withdrawal effects and legal retention obligations must be explained.
- No direct notification of superiors or other agency without lawfully authorized necessity.

## STOP criteria
No agency permission, unresolved IP rights, missing security approval, request for protected Defence data, unauthorized banking aggregation or unlicensed finance mediation.
