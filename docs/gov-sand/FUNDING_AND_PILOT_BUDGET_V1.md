# Funding pathways and staged proof budget (illustrative, not approved)
Priority: Ministry relationship first; funding applications in parallel only if legal entity and terms qualify.

## Gate-based budget example
| Stage | Cost SAR | Release gate |
|---|---:|---|
| Synthetic prototype and tests | 45,000 | reproducible test evidence |
| Security/privacy/legal review | 55,000 | documented clearance scope |
| Accessible Arabic UI and case tracking | 70,000 | user-test results |
| Pilot operations and training | 60,000 | written institutional sponsor |
| Governance and evidence audit | 30,000 | review signoff |
| Contingency | 40,000 | itemized approval |
| TOTAL | 300,000 | funding not secured |

## Funding options (verify current official terms on date of application)
1. Paid limited institutional pilot, if agency procurement permits. Never presume acceptance.
2. Social Development Bank entrepreneurship financing; 50,000–500,000 SAR and published fees must be compared with cashflow. Official: https://www.sdb.gov.sa/ar/enterprise-financing/entrepreneur-financing
3. Innovation grants subject to open calls and separate eligibility.
4. Corporate partner underwriting through transparent procurement/CSR terms.
5. Private capital after founder/IP/third-party ownership review.

## Investment committee conditions
No debt for a project unable to service repayments, no handling citizens' loan capital, no use of charitable money as speculative capital. All non-government financing to be separately approved and logged.
