# Defence / Eatizaz pilot readiness scorecard
NOT an official Defence scoring scheme. Internal gate, and no fictitious readiness percentage.

| Criterion | Weight | Verified status | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Legal entity / authorized signatory | 15 | UNKNOWN | Commercial record, authorization |
| Founder and third party IP rights | 10 | PENDING | License/source review |
| Runnable demo and tests | 20 | PENDING CI | Reproducible pass |
| Cybersecurity / privacy by design | 20 | DOCUMENTED, not assessed | DPIA, security tests |
| Measurable citizen/military service impact | 15 | HYPOTHESIS | Synthetic baseline then approved trial |
| Pilot scope and governance | 10 | DRAFT | Signed institution sponsor |
| Financial plan | 10 | PENDING | Budget / contract |

Do not equate sum of documentation with readiness or probability of acceptance.
Hard gate: no live pilot, real-person data or Ministry brand without proper permissions. The 100-point framework is only for internal prioritization.
