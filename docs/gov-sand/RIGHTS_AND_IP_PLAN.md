# Founder rights and IP protection plan — legal review required
Status: PLANNED, not legal certification.

1. Document ownership of VAIXLNS: entity vs individual, contributor agreements, employment/IP assignments, historical source provenance and OSS dependencies. Founder strategy control must not override legal duties or contractual rights.
2. Preserve complete Git history; timestamp SHA-256 of design artifacts and approved releases, keep private originals and proof of authorship separately. Git history alone is not conclusive proof of ownership.
3. Register eligible software/apps with Saudi Authority for Intellectual Property (SAIP) as appropriate. Official software copyright registration: https://www.saip.gov.sa/ar/services/copyrights/copyr1 . Registration helps evidence rights but does not turn an idea into exclusive ownership of a public service concept.
4. Trademark clearance, then registration for VAIXLNS/SAND marks if suitable, through official process. No Ministry of Defence, Eatizaz or government marks without permission.
5. Control technical demo disclosure: non-confidential executive one-pager first; confidential architecture/source code only under suitable written NDA and internal legal clearance. Do not assert all unsolicited government submissions are confidential.
6. Commercial proposal terms: background IP retained by owner; pilot grants limited non-exclusive right to evaluate, no ownership transfer; foreground IP, permitted reuse, confidentiality, indemnities, costs and data controller/processor roles explicitly negotiated.
7. Use private repository for sensitive business terms and never store IDs, sensitive personal records, Defence documents or API secrets in public GitHub.
8. Before offering a contract, verify commercial registration, authorized signatory and government procurement eligibility.
