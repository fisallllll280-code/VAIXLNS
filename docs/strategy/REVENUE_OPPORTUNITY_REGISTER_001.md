## Operating rule
A public website proves only what an organization says it does. It does not prove a budget, pain, buying authority, or intent to purchase. Do not mark any prospect qualified until direct evidence is collected. Do not contact anyone or publish outreach without human approval.

## Why these candidates
They publicly describe software delivery, integration, QA, ERP, infrastructure, or managed services—areas adjacent to a small engineering-verification offer. This is a relevance signal only, not evidence of unmet demand. Verify official URLs and service claims before any outreach.

## Offer to test (not yet validated)
**Release Evidence Sprint** — fixed-scope review of one authorized repository or integration:
1. Establish a baseline from permitted source and CI evidence.
2. Run only approved, non-destructive checks.
3. Report reproducible failures, dependency risks, and evidence gaps.
4. Deliver a prioritized remediation plan; implement fixes only under explicit scope.
5. Include hashes/logs and a clear PASS/FAIL/NOT RUN result per check.

Price hypothesis for testing: SAR 750–1,500 for a small diagnostic. Not a market-validated price or revenue forecast. Quote only after scope, access, and delivery cost are known.

## Qualification scorecard (0–2 each; require direct evidence)
- Confirmed pain
- Named owner / decision-maker
- Urgency and deadline
- Budget or paid-pilot willingness
- Access and delivery feasibility
- Repeat / referral potential

Maximum: 12. A website alone scores zero on pain, authority, urgency, and budget. No opportunity is “qualified” until a direct conversation or equivalent reliable evidence supports it.

## 72-hour evidence loop
- H0–H6: verify offer scope and produce a redacted sample report from a safe demo repo.
- H6–H24: validate 10–15 prospects from public sources; identify a specific, honest relevance angle for each.
- H24–H48: after approval, send a small batch of personalized messages; track delivered/replied/meeting/pilot.
- H48–H72: pursue one scoped paid pilot; record objections and actual conversion evidence.
- Stop or revise if there is no response signal; do not inflate counts or claim revenue.

## Required metrics
Prospects sourced; official source verified; relevance hypothesis; contact approved; outreach sent; replies; qualified conversations; paid pilots agreed; cash collected; delivery hours; direct costs; gross margin.

## Guardrails

- No claims of live agents, successful integrations, security certification, or revenue without direct evidence.
- No spending, money movement, external contact, or public posting without explicit authorization.
