Price hypotheses to test, not market facts: SAR 750–1,500 diagnostic; SAR 2,500–5,000 bounded remediation sprint. ## Specialist agents (contracts; not active until real provider execution is evidenced)
1. Opportunity Hunter — identify costly urgent problems and buyer roles from lawful public sources.
2. Buyer Evidence Investigator — separate buyer statements and primary evidence from inference; seek willingness-to-pay evidence.
3. Offer Architect — define scope, exclusions, acceptance criteria, turnaround, data/IP and rollback.
4. Pricing & Unit-Economics Analyst — model hours, acquisition costs, API/tool spend, fees, taxes/reserves, refunds, contribution margin and downside cases.
5. Demand Generation Operator — prepare personalized truthful outreach drafts; sending requires human approval.
6. Delivery Feasibility Engineer — inspect accessible tools/repositories and mark AVAILABLE / NEEDS_CONFIGURATION / NOT_AVAILABLE; never fake a live integration.
7. Adversarial Financial Reviewer — independently attack assumptions, pricing, legal/privacy risk, delivery burden and sales-cycle assumptions.
8. Revenue Controller — choose CONTINUE_TEST / REVISE / HOLD / STOP from evidence; cannot authorize payments, trades, contracts, publishing or self-promotion.

Every agent result must include task ID, role/version, provider/model if actually used, inputs, source URLs/date/digest, claims, counterevidence, unknowns, output artifact, limitations and next action. Missing provider => NOT_CONFIGURED/HOLD. No fabricated research, prospects, outreach, revenue or probability.

## 72-hour plan
### Hours 0–6: feasibility and offer
- Inspect existing VAIXLNS/VX assets and test paths; record exact accessible capabilities and gaps.
- Compare three offers against urgency, ability to deliver now, time to first paid pilot, repeatability, margin, acquisition friction and risk.
- Select one narrow offer only if evidence supports it.
- Produce a synthetic/redacted example deliverable; no customer data or secrets.

### Hours 6–24: buyer evidence
- Build a list of 10–15 relevant businesses using lawful public business sources.
- Record source/date, buyer role, evidence of pain, why this offer fits, and uncertainty.
- Draft five individually tailored outreach messages and a one-page offer.
- Do not send or publish without human approval. Likes, silence, AI agreement and competitor existence do not validate demand.

### Hours 24–48: real conversations
- Ask qualified prospects about current workflow, cost of failure, urgency, budget owner, access and acceptance criteria.
- Record actual responses; no response remains NO_RESPONSE.
- Revise one variable at a time (buyer, problem, scope, proof or price).
- Issue a written pilot proposal only when the buyer and delivery boundary are clear.

### Hours 48–72: close or learn
- Seek one paid, bounded pilot; contract and payment terms require human approval.
- If accepted, deliver only within authorized scope, with least-privilege access and auditable evidence.
- If not sold, report actual funnel outcomes, objections and the next test. Never describe an unsigned proposal as revenue.

## Target arithmetic (gross collected revenue, not profit)
- SAR 1,000/day averages SAR 30,000 over a 30-day month.
- 10 collected SAR 3,000 engagements/month = SAR 30,000 gross/month.
- 6 collected SAR 5,000 engagements/month = SAR 30,000 gross/month.
These are arithmetic scenarios, not a forecast. Required lead volume and probability must come from observed conversion data. Report collected cash, refunds, fees, delivery costs and contribution margin separately.

## Hard safety and truth gates
No autonomous banking access, trades, transfers, borrowing, ad spend, paid tools, contract signing or external messages. Human approval is required for customer contact, publication, commitments and real-money actions. No guarantee of earnings. If feasibility, authorization, privacy, licensing or economics fail, HOLD or STOP.
Success = primary buyer evidence, a bounded offer, independent unit economics review, truthful outreach/response record, explicit next action, and verifiable delivery status.
