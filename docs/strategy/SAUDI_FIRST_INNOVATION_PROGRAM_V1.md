The initial commercial milestone is **US$10,000 of founder-provided financing from the founder's personal portfolio/wallet**, used according to a documented sources-and-uses schedule. ## 2. Saudi-first engineering tool and service offer

The VAIXLNS tool/agent fabric should connect to authorized company repositories, APIs, documentation, workflows, and development environments through explicit contracts, least-privilege permissions, logging, and rollback. The protected VAIXLNS core is not a customer deliverable and is not disclosed by default.

### Initial service lanes

1. **Engineering baseline:** architecture/repository review, risk and dependency mapping, reproducible build/test setup, prioritized remediation plan.
2. **Startup workflow pilot:** one bounded automation or agent-assisted workflow with permissions, acceptance tests, logs, and rollback instructions.
3. **Infrastructure integration sprint:** a narrowly scoped integration, reliability/recovery improvement, or developer-productivity capability with repeatable test evidence.

Custody of money, discretionary trading, personalized investment recommendations, regulated payments, or promises of financial return are out of scope unless all required licensing, controls, contracts, and authorized counterparties are in place.

The first customers and use cases should be selected from validated demand and delivery evidence in Saudi Arabia. US firms are later commercial/technical prospects unless a real opportunity supports earlier engagement.

## 4. Founder financing: source, use, and obligations

### Source classification

For this plan, the US$10,000 is **proposed founder-provided capital from the founder's own portfolio/wallet**. Before any funds are treated as available, record the source, currency, actual amount, date, ownership, account/custodian evidence, and whether the correct legal/accounting classification is a founder contribution, shareholder/owner loan, or another permitted category. If the money is actually borrowed from a bank, record the bank facility, interest/fees, repayment schedule, and covenants; do not infer that fact.

### Uses-of-funds ledger

Before committing money, create a list of all due and essential obligations, amounts, due dates, payment evidence, and consequences of non-payment. Then separately estimate the capital required for engineering delivery, customer acquisition, hosting/tools, legal/accounting, refunds/chargebacks, tax, and contingency.

| Allocation envelope | Amount | Rule |
|---|---:|---|
| Due essential/personal obligations explicitly approved for payment | To be evidenced | Prioritize by due date and consequence; retain proof of payment |
| Required business/engineering launch expenses | To be budgeted | Only documented, scoped expenses |
| Taxes, refunds, disputes, and contractual reserves | To be budgeted | Keep accessible where needed to meet obligations |
| Innovation Fund 30-day restricted balance | Actual residual only | Lock only after prior envelopes are reconciled and funded |
| **Total** | **Must reconcile to actual available capital** | No double counting |

This is the critical cash rule: **the same dollars cannot both pay obligations and remain locked.** If the full US$10,000 is needed for due obligations and launch costs, no amount from that tranche should be described as locked. Instead, pay/earmark those obligations and commence the 30-day restriction only on a separately identified residual balance. If the founder's intent is to lock the entire US$10,000, all necessary payments must be funded from other money before the lock begins.

## 5. Thirty-day no-withdrawal rule

The intended restriction is **30 consecutive calendar days without founder distributions or discretionary withdrawals from the designated Innovation Fund reserve**.

### Lock trigger evidence

Start the clock only once all the following exist: (a) the amount designated for the Innovation Fund is actually available and cleared, (b) the obligation and operating envelopes are separately reconciled, (c) the reserve amount and fund/account identity are recorded, (d) a reconciled ledger and account/custodian record support the balance, and (e) the founder approves the lock event.

During the 30-day period:

- No founder distributions, personal use, discretionary loans, pledges, or ordinary operating expenditure from the locked reserve.
- A discretionary outflow decision is `DENY_LOCK_ACTIVE`; attempted movement is logged.
- Necessary obligations, taxes, refunds, chargebacks, payroll, and customer liabilities must be budgeted separately before locking. Do not lock funds that must remain available for these obligations.
- No software policy is described as bank-enforced unless the actual account/custodian and tested controls support that claim.
- Day 30 does not cause automatic release. Unlock requires founder approval, reconciliation, review of remaining obligations, and an auditable unlock event. The founder can extend the lock.

This operating policy does not override law, customer rights, banking/custodian terms, or licensing obligations.
