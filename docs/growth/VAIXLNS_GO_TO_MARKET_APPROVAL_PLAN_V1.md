## Channel purpose
- GitHub: canonical code, releases, test evidence, and technical documentation.
- LinkedIn: customer problems, partner conversations, engineering updates, and pilot invitations.
- X: concise development updates and links to reproducible evidence.
- YouTube: short product walkthroughs and reproducible demonstrations.
- Pinterest: evergreen architecture diagrams that link to canonical documentation.

## Operating cycle
1. Choose one customer problem and define the audience.
2. Prepare a demonstration and link each technical claim to repository evidence.
3. Draft platform-specific copy and creative assets.
4. Review accuracy, privacy, IP, security, destination, and call to action.
5. Obtain explicit human approval for the exact content and platform.
6. Publish through an authorized platform account or connected integration.
7. Record the final URL and measure qualified visits, demo requests, partner meetings, pilot starts, and revenue.

## Approval record
Every item records campaign ID, version, platform, account, exact copy, asset URL, claim evidence, audience, CTA, scheduled time and timezone, review result, approver, approval timestamp, final post URL, and metrics.

Statuses: DRAFT -> EVIDENCE_REVIEW -> HUMAN_APPROVAL_REQUIRED -> APPROVED -> PUBLISHED -> MEASURED. No automated publishing is enabled by this plan.
